Question 1: Leave Entitlements
Question: How many Earned Leaves (EL) and Casual/Sick Leaves (CL/SL) is a fresher entitled to according to the company policy?

Answer: A fresher accrues 1.5 Earned Leaves per month (only after the successful completion of the probation period) and is entitled to 12 days of consolidated Casual/Sick Leaves per calendar year (pro-rated based on the date of joining).

Reference: Section 03: Working Hours, Attendance & Leave Policy.

Question 2: Probation and Notice Period
Question: What is the standard duration of the probation period for freshers, and what is the notice period required if an employee wishes to resign during this time?

Answer: The standard probation period for freshers is 6 months. If an employee wishes to resign during probation, they must serve a 30-day notice period (which increases to 60 days post-confirmation).

Reference: Section 06: Probation Period & Performance Management AND Section 09: Separation, Exit Formalities & Declaration.

Question 3: IT Security Protocols
Question: How frequently are employees required to update their company account passwords?

Answer: Employees are required to update their strong, alphanumeric passwords every 90 days, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) must always remain enabled.

Reference: Section 05: IT Assets, Network Security & Device Usage.

Question 4: Employee Benefits
Question: What is the specific percentage of the Provident Fund (PF) contribution, and what is the coverage limit for the Group Medical Insurance?

Answer: Both the employee and the company contribute 12% of the Basic Salary towards the Provident Fund. The Group Medical Insurance policy provides hospitalization coverage of up to $50,000 annually.

Reference: Section 04: Compensation, Payroll & Benefits Administration.

Question 5: Professional Development
Question: Under the Learning, Development & Career Progression policy, how many certifications will the company sponsor for an employee, and what are the conditions?

Answer: The company will sponsor one industry-recognized technical or management certification per year, provided that it aligns with the employee's career trajectory and is pre-approved by their reporting manager.

Reference: Section 07: Learning, Development & Career Progression.
